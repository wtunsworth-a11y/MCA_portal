How this works. Usage events are logged server-side against the access tier. The public portal records only aggregate counts (visits, dataset views, searches, documents read) with no personal data. Registered and partner sign-ins are attributed to the named account so partner and donor reporting can show who used what, when. Figures on this page are illustrative; production feeds from the portal's event log and exports to CSV/PDF for reporting periods.
