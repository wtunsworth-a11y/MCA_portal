Site analysis

Define a site — draw it, drop a point, or upload a boundary — and get an instant profile: its area and how it sits against the Managalas Conservation Area and Oro Province. Satellite themes (forest loss, carbon, fire…) are added once Earth Engine is connected.

m
Point → click the map (buffered to the radius above). Polygon → click each corner, then Finish (or double-click). Upload → GeoJSON or KML. Everything is computed in your browser; nothing is uploaded or stored.

What a full site profile will include

The pilot computes area and boundary overlap now; these satellite themes follow once Earth Engine is connected.

Topography

Elevation, slope & hillshade — min / mean / max.

Land cover

Land-cover map and the % in each class.

Forest cover & loss

Forest extent and ha / % lost over time.

Canopy & carbon

Canopy height, biomass and carbon stock.

Fire history

Active-fire detections, counted by year.

Access & people

Travel time and population near the site.

🔒
Nothing is stored. The site you draw or upload is analysed in your browser and is not saved to the portal or shown to other users. Report export is by Print / Save as PDF; email delivery arrives with the full version.
