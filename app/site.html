Define a site — draw it, drop a point, or upload a boundary — and get an instant profile: its area, how it sits against the Managalas Conservation Area and Oro Province, an EUDR deforestation screen, and forest, land-cover and fire themes from satellite data. EUDR deforestation screen — how to read it

When you define a plot, the screen estimates how much of it falls into three categories, using the JRC GFC2020 forest-2020 benchmark and Hansen tree-cover loss after the cut-off:

  • Standing forest — forest in 2020 that is still standing. Deforestation-free.
  • Pre-2020 non-forest — already non-forest in 2020 (existing gardens, settlements, grassland). Not “deforestation” under the EUDR.
  • Cleared since 2020 — forest in 2020 cleared after the 31 Dec 2020 cut-off. This is the EUDR concern.
Verdict grade on the cleared share: 🟢 0% · 🟡 under 5% · 🔴 5% or more.
A screening indication to guide due diligence — not a legal EUDR determination. Based on global Hansen/JRC products (JRC states GFC2020 has no authoritative status); clearings mapped at ≥ 2 ha; confirm with plot-level and official checks before any sourcing decision.
