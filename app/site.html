Site analysis

Pick any site in Oro Province — draw it on the map or upload a boundary — and the portal generates a short landscape profile: a series of maps and summary statistics. A point becomes an area with a buffer. Think of it as a mini-MLA for your site.

Draw a site on the map → How it works

How it works

Four steps, from an area of interest to a site report.

1 · Define the site

Draw a point, line or polygon on the map, or upload a boundary (GeoJSON, KML, GPX or shapefile).

2 · Buffer a point

A point or line is buffered to an area — default 500 m, adjustable (about 250 m – 5 km). Polygons are used as drawn.

3 · Analyse

The portal computes each theme over the site and returns a map plus a statistic or two. Runs as a background job.

4 · Site report

Maps and stat cards on screen, plus an exportable PDF / DOCX for registered and partner users.

What you get for a site

A map and summary for each theme, clipped to your area of interest.

Topography

Elevation, slope & hillshade — min / mean / max.

Land cover

Land-cover map and the % in each class.

Forest cover & loss

Forest extent and ha / % lost over time.

Canopy & carbon

Canopy height, biomass and carbon stock.

Fire history

Active-fire detections, counted by year.

Climate

Rainfall and temperature summary for the site.

Hydrology

Catchments and rivers within and around the site.

Access & people

Travel time and population near the site.

Overlaps

Does the site intersect the MCA, a KBA or a protected area? Restricted overlaps (SABL / PFMP / mining) show only for authorised users.

🔒
Access & privacy. Public users get the public theme set over a capped area; registered and partner users get the full set and report export. Sensitive overlaps appear only for users who hold that access group. A site you draw or upload is your own — it is kept to your session or account and never shown to other users.

This tool is planned for Phase 4b. It reuses the portal's Earth Engine layers and the project's landscape-analysis method, so nothing new needs to be downloaded or stored to run it.
